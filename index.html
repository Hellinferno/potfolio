/ 01 About & Engineering Journey

Grounded AI systems, verified by deterministic code.

I am pursuing a B.Sc. in Applied AI and Data Science at IIT Jodhpur (2025–2029) alongside my CA Intermediate (ICAI) qualification. That dual training shapes how I design software: I treat probabilistic language models as orchestrators that must be anchored to deterministic verification layers.

Across my recent builds, that principle shows up everywhere—backing an AI tutor with float64 NumPy invariants in ClaimLab, gating incident remediation behind GRAPH.COPY counterfactual sandboxes in AegisGraph, enforcing AST-allowlisted python -I -S sandboxes with zero false-verified answers in StudyLab, and logging every valuation calculation into an immutable audit trail in FinMod-Agent.

Outside AI pipelines, I also ship full-stack and systems software in TypeScript, React 19, FastAPI, FalkorDB, SQLite/D1, and Rust, with automated CI suites and reproducible local test harnesses.
